\caption{\textbf{Model-based position quality during Premier League high presses.} \textbf{(a)} Schematic of the counterfactual positioning experiment. We move the recorded presser to nearby positions while holding the other players fixed. For each valid alternative, we rebuild the structural pressure network and predict the probability of a controlled regain within five seconds. Position quality is the predicted probability at the observed position minus the mean across valid alternatives, expressed in percentage points. \textbf{(b)} Club mean position quality versus 2025/26 season points for all 20 Premier League clubs. The line shows a least-squares fit. The annotation gives Spearman's $\rho$ and its 95\% bootstrap interval from $10{,}000$ draws of whole fixtures. \textbf{(c)} The ten highest player means among the 181 players with at least 15 eligible high presses and at least $1{,}350$ Premier League minutes (15 full-match equivalents) that season.}
